### 🔧 What I'm building

**[`admin-lab`](https://github.com/rauf-babajanov/admin-lab)** — a real Windows endpoint/identity lab, not a tutorial I followed once and forgot.

It's still early — right now it's got a proper repo structure, a documented decision on why I built it the way I did, and the first real milestone closed. Where it's headed: a working domain, a couple of managed endpoints, policies that work and a few I break on purpose just to practice fixing them properly.

The thing I care about most isn't finishing fast — it's that every step leaves a trail:

- **why** I made a call, not just what I clicked
- **proof** it actually worked — real command output, not "trust me"
- when something breaks on purpose, the full story: what I saw, what I guessed wrong, what was actually true

It's private while it's still a skeleton. Goes public once it's actually worth someone's time to read.

### 🧠 How I actually work

- If I can't show you output proving it works, I don't consider it working yet.
- I make one change at a time, and I always know how to undo it.
- When something's broken, I check the network, then DNS, then auth, then policy — in that order, not by guessing.
- If I didn't write it down, I probably didn't actually learn it.
